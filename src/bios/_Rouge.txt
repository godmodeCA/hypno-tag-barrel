[BIO]
Rouge the Bat tends to be a formidable presence that commands attention wherever she finds herself. Her ability makes for breeding grounds of the attention she so desparately craves, turning her into a magnet for admiration and desire. With her striking appearance and irresistible allure, Rouge naturally becomes the focal point, effortlessly ensnaring those around her.

However, Rouge's greatest strength is also her Achilles' heel. Her insatiable jealousy and vanity often cloud her judgment, leading to a relentless pursuit of control and dominance. This intense emotion drives her to ensnare men into slavery, especially when her desires for jewelry, gadgetry, or treasure peak. Her jealous nature can manifest in unpredictable and often dangerous ways, causing conflicts and misunderstandings that hinder her personal growth.

Despite these flaws, Rouge's skills and talents are undeniably impressive. Her size, softness, and mommy attributes are all top-tier, and her hypnotic abilities further enhance her charm, allowing her to captivate and control those around her. Her ability is well suited to boosting her hypnotic influence, making her a well-rounded and dangerous adversary.

Rouge's journey is one of balancing her insatiable desire for control and attraction with the need for personal growth and emotional stability. Her vanity and jealousy often lead her down a path of manipulation and enslavement, but her charm and allure ensure that she remains a memorable and impactful figure, leaving a lasting impression on all who cross her path.
